Details

—

Routes and contract addresses mirror https://bridge.zaphod.one/api/state (fetched 2026-11-07). Sends from 42 Chain / BSC / Ethereum go through the Zaphod BridgeRouter: approve the token to the BridgeRouter, then transferRemote with the messaging fee from a fresh quoteNative call paid on top. Sends from QL1 have no BridgeRouter: the token is approved to the Hyperlane warp router and transferRemote is called with value 0. Arrival is checked on the destination chain's Hyperlane mailbox (delivered(messageId)) plus the recipient's balance.

Experimental fee-free route (42 Chain → BSC / Ethereum only, optional and clearly labelled): approves the token to the token's Hyperlane warp router and calls transferRemote with value 0, bypassing the BridgeRouter — so the usual 2 USDT/USDC fee is skipped. Measured live on 2026-11-07: the USDT/USDC/WBTC warp routers' mailbox is the Infinite mailbox, their hook is the zero address, and quoteGasPayment is 0 for both destinations. Delivery this way is NOT proven yet — that is what a first small test send will settle. If it does not arrive, the sent amount may be stuck. The standard route above remains the default.

Disabled after live checks on 2026-11-07: sending FROM BNB Smart Chain and FROM Ethereum (their BridgeRouters are older versions — 1.1.0 / 1.2.0 — and their messaging-fee quote reverts, so the fee cannot be checked before sending; receiving on those chains works), and OSAK from 42 Chain to QL1 (that direction has zero sends in the contract's whole history — unproven). Every other route was verified live: contract version, paused flag, token address, allowed destination, minimum/maximum and both quotes answered.
